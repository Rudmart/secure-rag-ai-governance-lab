Fictional AI Governance Policy

AI System Registration
All AI systems used by the fictional organization must be registered before deployment. The registry must identify the system owner, intended purpose, and data sources.

Risk Assessment
Each AI system must undergo a risk assessment covering potential harm, bias, privacy, and reliability. System owners must document safeguards and reassess risks whenever the system's purpose or capabilities change.

Human Approval
A designated human reviewer must approve each AI system before it enters operational use. Decisions that significantly affect individuals must receive human review, with authority to override or stop the system.
